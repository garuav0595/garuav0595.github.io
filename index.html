Who I Am

I'm a DevOps Engineer at Sarv.com, focused on Kubernetes (EKS), Docker, GitOps, and CI/CD. I care about the boring, load-bearing parts of infrastructure — the parts that make a 2am rollback a 5-minute non-event instead of a 2-hour incident.

My work spans provisioning infrastructure with Terraform, automating deployments with Jenkins & ArgoCD, hardening clusters with Kustomize & kubeadm, and keeping systems observable with Prometheus, Grafana & the ELK stack.

I also write — turning what I learn on the job into public write-ups on LinkedIn, then into working, open-source reference projects on GitHub.
